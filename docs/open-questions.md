## 6. Publication and approval rules

- Draft content is not treated as approved catalog content.
- A rubric cannot be published until all five rating levels, weights, gates and correction templates validate and role expert approval is recorded.
- Role expert approval is required for role-specific learning content and rubric criteria.
- Product owner approval is required for product policy and scope decisions.
- QA tests count as executed only when the platform actually runs them.
- UX AI findings remain advisory; trainer approval is mandatory.
- There is no demo history. Evaluation records come only from the real evaluation pipeline (superseded rule: demo history through the pipeline, 8 Nov 2026).
- Published rubric versions are immutable and identified by content hash.

### 7.4 Data handling statement for AI-assisted authoring (A5, closes open decision 8 for authoring only)

Stated by the product owner, 8 November 2026. Drafted by the engineering assistant at the product owner's request. Not yet reviewed by anyone else.

- Provider: OpenAI, through the LLM gateway only.
- Allowed to send: course topics, trainer notes, and uploaded course files.
- Not allowed to send: intern personal data. The authoring features take no intern data as input.
- Provider terms: the OpenAI account is to be configured so that data is not used to train models.

Open items:
- The "no training" account setting is the product owner's statement. Nobody has verified it in the OpenAI account, and the repository cannot check it. Verify it before setting `LLM_LIVE_APPROVED=true` in any shared environment.
- This statement covers authoring only. Sending intern submissions to the provider for evaluation is a separate case and stays open under decision 8.
- Review: a second person (tech lead or product owner delegate) should confirm this statement.
